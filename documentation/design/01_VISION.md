# Eternal Guild — Vision & Pillars

**One page. Evergreen. Re-read this when scope creep or competitor anxiety hits.**
*Last updated: 2026-06-09. Supersedes 00/01/02/04/07 GDD prose.*

---

## The game in three sentences

You are a retired adventurer running a tavern, sending younger adventurers on
dangerous missions and living with the results. The emotional core is the
end-of-day reveal — watching who came back and who didn't. Cozy management on
the surface, dark-fantasy emotional weight underneath.

## The fantasy

**Enabler of heroes.** The player never descends. They pour the drinks, light the
fire, choose who walks out the door — and they are complicit in what comes back.
The personal descent exists only as a late-game narrative capstone, earned by a
whole campaign of staying upstairs.

## Pillars (in priority order)

1. **Complicity & Consequence.** Every other system exists to set up the
   end-of-day reveal. The subject of every emotional beat is the *adventurer*,
   never the mission. Loss is meaningful but not punishing — memorialization and
   specific consequences, never total setbacks.
2. **Deep Character Investment.** Adventurers are evolving individuals (Journey
   Through the Arcana), not stats. 22 Major Arcana as named story adventurers,
   56 Minor Arcana as supporting cast. Economy is the natural roster limiter.
3. **Meaningful Management.** "Meaningful pressure creates engaging stories."
   Taxes, firewood, beer, wages — pressure forces choices, choices create stories.
4. **Curiosity & Discovery.** The Priors, their teal-rune ruins, Den Fa's secrets.
   Mystery is an asset: the player never sees what happened on a mission, only
   who came back and how they look.
5. **Legacy.** One adventurer becomes the next run's protagonist, opening a new
   tavern in a different settlement. **Max 10 settlements.** The hourglass loop,
   not infinite proceduralism, is the replayability story.

## Tone ceiling

Grimgar-style melancholy. Weathered and tired, never glamorous, never gory.
Cosy does not mean stakeless — it means the warmth and the weight share the same
room (Spiritfarer register). The fire is lit, the mead is warm, and someone
didn't come back tonight.

## Visual identity

Pixel-on-3D hybrid (HD-2D). Ghibli warmth inside the tavern; Mignola/Berserk
stark shadow geometry outside. KayKit assets under the pixel post-process
shader. Tarot portrait art (pixel + Ghibli warmth + Berserk shadow, RWS-faithful)
is the character identity across roster, reveal, and Arcana progression.

## What this game is NOT

- Not a battle-report reader. Mission detail stays off-screen, forever.
- Not an open-ended procedural sandbox. Ten settlements, one fixed map per run.
- Not a hero RPG. The player stays upstairs.
- Not gore-dark. Melancholy, not misery.

## Audience

Players of RimWorld, Darkest Dungeon, Stardew Valley, Spiritfarer — people who
want management with a heart and consequences with a face.
